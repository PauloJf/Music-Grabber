# streamrip 2.1.0 (the latest release) requires Pillow<11, and 10.4 has dozens of known advisories
# (docs/security.md, finding 3). Music Grabber uses Pillow for cover art, and streamrip only for its
# Deezer client (its own Pillow code, a cover-downscale helper, is never called). Tests and the
# Deezer client import were checked on Pillow 13. pip prints a "dependency conflict" note for
# streamrip when installing this; that is expected and exits 0. It can't be one command: pip would
# refuse to resolve the two requirements together.
pillow>=12.3
